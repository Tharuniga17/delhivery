Module 08

Key Scheduled Jobs Overview

All automated background jobs powering the system — their triggers, frequencies, and what they do.

rmq-auto-create-driver-attendance
📅 Daily ⏰ 10:00 AM RabbitMQ
Mark auto-attendance for eligible drivers. Creates today's attendance if last status is Available, Paid Leave, or Driver Training — with skip conditions applied.
rmq-driver-absent-create
📅 Daily ⏰ 10:00 AM RabbitMQ
Mark absent if no attendance for 3+ days. Triggers absent (7) status when last attendance date is more than 72 hours ago.
rmq-second-driver-attendance-create
📅 Daily ⏰ 10:00 AM RabbitMQ
Create attendance when a driver is mapped to a trip. Fires when a driver is assigned, requiring an attendance record to be created or updated.
rmq-div-attendance-create
⚡ Real Time ⏰ Instant Event-Driven
Update attendance on driver status updates and trip close. Real-time event-driven synchronization for immediate state consistency.
rmq-div-weekly-driver-compoff-update
📅 Weekly ⏰ Mon 10:30 AM Comp Off
Generate comp off for Sunday work. Credits one comp-off to any driver who worked on Sunday or a national holiday in the past week.
rmq-div-msl-driver-compoff-reset
📅 Daily ⏰ Real Time Comp Off
Expire comp off after 30 days. Checks all expiry dates and resets available comp off balance when records have passed their expiry date.
rmq-div-driver-compoff-encash
📅 Monthly ⏰ 10:10 AM Comp Off
Encash extra comp off above 4 units for Volvo category drivers. Excess balance is encashed and a salary credit is triggered automatically.
rmq-div-driver-leave-log-update
⚡ Real Time ⏰ Instant Event-Driven
Track comp off log on utilization (Done rule). Updates driver leave log entries when comp off is manually utilized by a manager.
